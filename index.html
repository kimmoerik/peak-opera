Performing at the International Gilbert & Sullivan Festival

Who We Are

We are Peak Opera. We are an amateur society who perform at the International Gilbert and Sullivan Festival every year. Everybody learns their music at home, then we meet up for a week of rehearsals and finish off the week by performing the show in the Buxton Opera House.

Upcoming Events

Our standard event is performing at the International Gilbert and Sullivan Festival. The show we will be performing in 2027 has not yet been announced. But when it is, we will be sure to update! Our performance and rehearsal dates will also be released shortly.
