Who We Are

We are Peak Opera. We are a amature society who perform at the International Gilbert and Sullivan Festival every year. Everybody learns there music at home. Then we meet up for a week of rehersals and finish off the week by performing the show in the Buxton Opera House.

Upcoming Events

Our standard event is performing at the International Gilbert and Sullivan Festival. The show we will be performing 2027 has not yet been anounced. But when it is we will be sure to update! Our performance and rehersal dates will also be released shortly.
